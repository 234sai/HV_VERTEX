About Company

Bridging Hardware With Future Intelligence

"Technology should make life easier, not more complicated." At HV Vertex Electronics, we follow a rigorous problem-to-solution approach. We build intelligent retrofit systems, autonomous robotics, and reliable components that turn tedious manual routines into automated simplicity.

Our Vision

To build a smarter, easier, and more connected future through accessible electronics and intelligent automation for homes, colleges, and small industries.

Our Mission

Design, develop, manufacture, and supply reliable, affordable Arduino & ESP32 products, motor drivers, robotics systems, and energy-saving automation.

Future Scope & R&D
