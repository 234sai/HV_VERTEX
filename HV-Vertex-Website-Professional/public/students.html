All orders are processed rapidly. Standard courier delivery takes 2 to 4 business days depending on your city/state across India.

Yes! For batch requirements of 5+ units or lab equipment setups, we offer subsidized bulk pricing. Contact our engineering desk at hr@hvvertex.in.
